# 9. Case study

This section is reserved for one worked example. The example will show the method from start to end:

- Seed selection, with a basis statement for each account.
- Scope.
- Collection.
- The archive.
- Feature extraction.
- Attribution.
- The evidence packet.
- Use in an adversarial setting.

The example is not in this version. The reasons come from the ethical rules of the method. They are not a delay for convenience.

## Why this section is currently a placeholder

The author has one real investigation in mind. The method was built on that investigation. It has real accounts, real signals, real claims, and real later effects. A publishable example from that investigation needs work that is not done.

**Anonymization at the method's own standard.** §3.3.3 limits attribution to a cluster. The example must not name a natural person, even if that person is public. The example must show cluster labels, which is the form the method itself produces. That anonymization is not done.

**The audience exclusions.** §1.2 and §10.2 name populations the method refuses to target. The raw investigation may touch people next to the network who are in those populations. A published example must not surface that incidental information. That review is not done.

**Incidental findings.** §10.5 keeps an investigation inside its stated scope. The example must show what was collected and not analyzed, what was analyzed and not published, and what was published because it supports the claim. That selection is not done.

**The operator will read the example.** §8.4 says the operator of an attributed network will read the paper and the findings. A real example will be read by that operator. The method accepts that reading. §8.4.2 covers the usual replies. An early example can also expose tactics from an investigation that is still open. The author has not made that timing judgment.

## What the case study will contain when complete

The example will follow the structure this paper already fixes. The structure is fixed now so the case is not reshaped later to fit the method.

The example will include:

- The basis statement for each seed account, with cluster labels, and with the operational reason for inclusion.
- The scope decisions (time bounds, account scope, content scope) and the reason recorded at the time.
- The collection summary (which collector, what was archived, the manifest signature).
- A sample of the signal table, with provenance back to archived artifacts, for each signal category in §4.
- The attribution output at the band the signals support, plus the alternative explanations the reasoner had to consider.
- The evidence packet, and the redaction, format, and signature used at publication.
- The practitioner's note on what the method showed, what it declined, and what it missed.

The example will be one investigation. It will not claim a general result. Readers who want more examples should use the invitation below.

## Open invitation to community case studies

Case studies from other practitioners are welcome. Publish them as separate works that cite this paper. Follow the structure above. Respect the anonymization rules.

The author will review a proposed case against the method's rules. Treat the author as a reviewer, not as a co-author. The case remains the contributor's work.

The reference-implementation repository may link to a case that meets the rules. A link means the case follows the method. It does not mean the author endorses the contributor's view of that investigation.

## Reading without the case study

The method specification is complete without the reserved case. A synthetic example is in `examples/synthetic-network-case-study.md`.

Apply the method from §3 through §8, and apply the ethical rules in §10. The reference implementation is the operational form of this paper. Use the implementation, the synthetic example, and this specification together.

When the real case is ready, a later version of the paper will add it and will record the version change. This placeholder states the intent. It does not promise a date. The method is published because the method is complete. The case is not a dependency of the specification.
